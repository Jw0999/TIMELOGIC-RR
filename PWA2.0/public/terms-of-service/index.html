3. Attendance Integrity & Anti-Proxy Policies

Users must submit accurate and authentic attendance logs. 4. Biometric Face Verification

Facial authentication is used as an identity verification mechanism. Biometric landmark vectors are analyzed solely for matching against registered employee profiles to verify active presence at the designated physical kiosk terminal.

5. Offline Resilience & Synchronization

In the event of network disruption, this kiosk terminal operates in resilient local offline mode. Attendance punches stored in local cache will synchronize with the enterprise database upon connection restoration. The authoritative clock time recorded corresponds to the verified time stamp established by the station.
