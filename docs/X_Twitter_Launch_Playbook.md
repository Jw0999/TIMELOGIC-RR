## 3. High-Velocity Engagement Rules

### Rule 1: The 1-on-1 Outreach (Do this in the first 2 hours)
Send this direct DM to 20–30 developer friends, colleagues, or founders on WhatsApp, Telegram, or LinkedIn:
> *"Hey! We just went live with TimeLogic on Product Hunt today. We're competing for Product of the Day and it would mean the world if you could check out our page and drop some feedback/support: [LINK]"*

### Rule 2: Never Say "Upvote"
The Product Hunt spam filter flags messages containing the word *"upvote"*. Always use:
- *"Check out our launch"*
- *"Drop your feedback / support our launch"*
- *"Join the discussion"*

### Rule 3: The 15-Minute Reply Rule
Every time someone leaves a comment on your Product Hunt or Twitter thread:
- Reply within **15 minutes**.
- Always ask a question back (e.g., *"Thanks! How does your company currently track shift attendance?"*).
- Active conversation threads signal high engagement to the ranking algorithm.
