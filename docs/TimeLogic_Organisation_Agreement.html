Organisation Registration & Service Agreement

This Agreement is entered into between TimeLogic Technologies ("TimeLogic", "the Platform", "the Provider") and the Organisation named herein ("the Client", "the Organisation"). By completing registration on the TimeLogic platform — whether through the web dashboard, mobile application, or authorised representative — the Organisation acknowledges that it has read, understood, and agrees to be bound by the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

01

Preamble & Background

TimeLogic Technologies is a technology company incorporated and operating under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, specialising in biometric attendance management, workforce intelligence, and cloud-based payroll integration. TimeLogic operates a hardware-anchored attendance kiosk platform combining edge facial-recognition hardware, offline data synchronisation, and a real-time cloud dashboard accessible to authorised administrators and employees.

This Agreement establishes the legal basis upon which any Organisation may access, subscribe to, and operate the TimeLogic platform. It defines the rights and obligations of both parties with respect to data handling, service levels, acceptable use, intellectual property, and dispute resolution. This document is legally binding and shall be governed by Nigerian law.

Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall bear the meanings assigned to them below unless the context otherwise requires:

  1. "Platform" — The TimeLogic web dashboard, mobile application, API endpoints, biometric kiosk hardware, and all associated software and services provided by TimeLogic Technologies.

  3. "Employee / Staff" — Any individual enrolled by the Organisation on the TimeLogic platform for the purpose of attendance tracking, shift management, or payroll integration.

  7. "Processing" — Any operation performed on Personal Data including collection, storage, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, or erasure.
  8. "Effective Date" — The date on which the Organisation completes registration on the TimeLogic platform or signs this Agreement, whichever is earlier.

Data We Collect

TimeLogic collects and processes the following categories of data in order to deliver, maintain, and improve the Platform. The Organisation is responsible for ensuring that all enrolled individuals have been informed of, and have consented to, the collection of the data types listed herein.

Category Data Types Collected Purpose
BIOMETRIC Facial recognition vector embeddings (mathematical representations); liveness detection metadata Identity verification at attendance kiosk; anti-spoofing
ATTENDANCE Clock-in / clock-out timestamps; shift records; late arrivals; absences; break durations Attendance tracking; payroll calculation; workforce analytics
EMPLOYEE Full name; employee ID; department; role; contact email; employment type Account management; payroll integration; reporting
DEVICE Kiosk device ID; firmware version; sync logs; location metadata (where enabled) Device health monitoring; offline sync reconciliation
ORGANISATION Organisation name; address; CAC number; billing information; administrator details Account registration; subscription management; compliance
USAGE Platform login events; feature usage logs; API call records; session metadata Platform security; performance optimisation; audit trails

Note on Biometric Data: TimeLogic stores only mathematical vector representations of facial features — not raw photographic images — in encrypted form. These vectors cannot be reverse-engineered to reconstruct a facial photograph. Original capture images are processed at the edge device and are not transmitted to or retained on TimeLogic servers.

Data Privacy Commitment — We Do Not Sell Your Data

ABSOLUTE COMMITMENT: TimeLogic Technologies does NOT sell, rent, trade, barter, or otherwise transfer Personal Data or Biometric Data belonging to the Organisation or its employees to any third party for commercial, marketing, or any other purpose. This commitment is unconditional and applies regardless of the subscription tier, contract value, or any other commercial consideration.

TimeLogic further commits to the following privacy principles:

  1. No Third-Party Advertising: TimeLogic does not use Organisation or employee data to serve targeted advertising on behalf of any third party, nor does it share data with advertising networks or data brokers.
  2. Limited Disclosure: Data shall only be disclosed to third parties where strictly necessary to operate the Platform (e.g., cloud infrastructure providers under binding data processing agreements), where required by a valid court order or applicable Nigerian law, or where the Organisation provides explicit written consent.
  3. Sub-Processor Accountability: Where TimeLogic engages third-party sub-processors (such as cloud hosting providers), those sub-processors shall be bound by contractual data protection obligations no less stringent than those in this Agreement.
  4. Employee Data Ownership: The Organisation retains ownership of all employee data submitted to the Platform. TimeLogic processes this data solely as a data processor acting on the Organisation's instructions.
  5. Data Access by TimeLogic Staff: Access to Organisation data by TimeLogic personnel is restricted to authorised support and engineering staff on a need-to-know basis, subject to internal access controls and audit logging.
06

Data Protection & Legal Compliance

6.1 — Applicable Legal Framework

Both parties agree to comply with the following applicable laws and regulations in their processing of personal data under this Agreement:

  • Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019, as issued by the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA)
  • Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023
  • The Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended) with respect to the right to privacy
  • Any other applicable sector-specific data protection regulations in force in Nigeria

6.2 — Organisation's Data Protection Responsibilities

  1. The Organisation shall act as Data Controller for all employee personal data submitted to the Platform and shall ensure that it has a valid lawful basis for processing under the NDPA 2023 (including obtaining informed consent from employees where required).
  2. The Organisation shall maintain a current employee data privacy notice informing enrolled staff of the nature, purpose, and duration of data processing conducted via TimeLogic.
  3. The Organisation shall promptly notify TimeLogic (within 48 hours of becoming aware) of any suspected breach of employee data security, accidental disclosure, or unauthorised access.
  4. The Organisation shall not enrol any minor (person under 18 years of age) on the Platform without appropriate legal guardianship consent and documentation.

6.3 — Data Retention & Deletion

  1. TimeLogic retains data for the duration of the active subscription period plus a maximum of 90 days following subscription expiry or account closure (the "Retention Window"), to allow the Organisation to export records.
  2. Upon written request received before the Retention Window expires, TimeLogic shall permanently delete all Organisation data within 30 business days.
  3. Biometric vector data shall be deleted immediately upon an employee's de-registration from the Platform or upon receipt of a verified deletion request from the Organisation.
  4. Certain anonymised, aggregated, and non-identifiable usage metrics may be retained by TimeLogic beyond the Retention Window solely for platform improvement purposes.

Organisation's Obligations & Responsibilities

By registering on the TimeLogic platform, the Organisation accepts the following obligations:

  1. Accurate Information: The Organisation shall provide accurate, complete, and up-to-date registration information and shall promptly update such information in the event of any change. Providing false or misleading information constitutes grounds for immediate account suspension.
  2. Account Security: The Organisation is solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of all administrator login credentials and API keys. The Organisation shall promptly notify TimeLogic of any unauthorised access or suspected breach of its account.
  3. Authorised Use Only: The Organisation shall use the Platform exclusively for its internal workforce management and attendance tracking operations. The Platform shall not be used to monitor individuals outside the Organisation's employment relationship without their documented consent.
  4. Device Integrity: The Organisation shall not tamper with, modify, reverse-engineer, or attempt to circumvent security mechanisms in any TimeLogic kiosk hardware or software provided under this Agreement. Physical damage or unauthorised modification of kiosk hardware voids warranty and may result in additional charges.
  5. Employee Consent: Prior to enrolling any employee's biometric data, the Organisation shall obtain documented informed consent in compliance with NDPA 2023 requirements. TimeLogic reserves the right to request evidence of consent at any time.
  6. Lawful Payroll Deductions: Where the Platform is used in conjunction with payroll systems, the Organisation is solely responsible for ensuring that any deductions from employee wages — whether for lateness, absence, or other reasons — comply with the Nigerian Labour Act (Cap L1, LFN 2004) and applicable employment contracts. TimeLogic provides tools for recording attendance data; the legal validity of resulting payroll deductions is the Organisation's sole responsibility.
  7. Subscription Payments: The Organisation shall pay all applicable subscription fees in accordance with the selected plan and payment schedule. Continued access to the Platform is conditional upon payment remaining current.
  8. Compliance with Laws: The Organisation shall, at all times, ensure its use of the Platform complies with all applicable Nigerian laws and regulations, including but not limited to employment law, data protection law, and anti-discrimination legislation.
08

TimeLogic's Obligations to the Organisation

  1. Service Availability: TimeLogic shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain Platform uptime of 99.5% or greater on a rolling monthly basis, excluding scheduled maintenance windows (which shall be communicated with a minimum of 24 hours' notice).
  2. Data Security: TimeLogic shall implement and maintain industry-standard technical and organisational security measures including encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+, AES-256), role-based access controls, regular vulnerability assessments, and incident response procedures.
  3. Customer Support: TimeLogic shall provide responsive technical support via email (support@timelogics.tech) and the in-platform support portal during designated support hours. Enterprise plan subscribers receive priority support with a target response time of 4 business hours.
  4. Transparency: TimeLogic shall notify the Organisation of any material changes to this Agreement or its privacy practices with a minimum of 30 days' advance notice via email or in-platform notification.
  5. Breach Notification: In the event of a confirmed data breach affecting the Organisation's data, TimeLogic shall notify the affected Organisation within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, in compliance with NDPA 2023 notification requirements.
  6. Data Portability: Upon request, TimeLogic shall provide the Organisation with an export of all its data in a machine-readable format (CSV/JSON) within 10 business days.
09

Acceptable Use & Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited and constitute grounds for immediate account suspension and potential legal action:

  1. Using the Platform to collect biometric data without the explicit, documented consent of the individuals concerned
  2. Sharing TimeLogic administrator credentials with unauthorised third parties or competitors
  3. Attempting to extract, scrape, or reverse-engineer biometric models or Platform algorithms
  4. Using the Platform to discriminate against employees on grounds prohibited by Nigerian law including gender, ethnicity, religion, disability, or pregnancy
  5. Misrepresenting attendance data, manipulating records, or instructing employees to fraudulently circumvent the biometric system
  6. Reselling, sub-licensing, or white-labelling the TimeLogic Platform or any component thereof without express written authorisation from TimeLogic Technologies
  7. Using the Platform for purposes unrelated to legitimate employment or workforce management
  8. Introducing malicious code, viruses, or any disruptive software into the Platform or connected systems
10

Subscription Terms & Termination

10.1 — Subscription Plans

The Organisation's access to the Platform is governed by the chosen Subscription Plan (Starter, Professional, or Enterprise). The features, employee limits, and pricing applicable to each plan are as set out in the current TimeLogic Monetisation and Access Policy document (TL-MAP-2024), which forms an annexure to this Agreement.

10.2 — Termination by the Organisation

The Organisation may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing 30 days' written notice to TimeLogic at support@timelogics.tech. Upon termination, the Organisation may request export of all its data within the 90-day Retention Window.

10.3 — Termination by TimeLogic

TimeLogic reserves the right to suspend or terminate the Organisation's account immediately, with or without prior notice, in the following circumstances:

  1. Material breach of any provision of this Agreement
  2. Non-payment of subscription fees after 14 days' written notice
  3. Confirmed misuse of data, biometric information, or Platform features
  4. Discovery that the Organisation provided materially false registration information
  5. Legal order or regulatory directive requiring discontinuation of service

10.4 — Effect of Termination

Upon termination, all licences granted to the Organisation under this Agreement shall immediately cease. TimeLogic shall retain data solely within the Retention Window as specified in Clause 6.3, after which all Organisation data shall be permanently deleted.

Confidentiality

Dispute Resolution

  1. Good Faith Negotiation: In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the parties shall first attempt to resolve the matter through good-faith negotiation for a period of 30 calendar days from the date either party gives written notice of the dispute to the other.
  2. Mediation: If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation, either party may refer the matter to mediation administered by the Lagos Multi-Door Courthouse (LMDC) or any other mutually agreed mediation body.
  3. Arbitration: If mediation fails to resolve the dispute within 60 days of referral, the dispute shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act (ACA) Cap A18, LFN 2004, or its successor legislation, conducted in Lagos, Nigeria, in the English language.
  4. Jurisdiction: Nothing in this clause shall prevent either party from seeking interim or emergency relief from a court of competent jurisdiction.
