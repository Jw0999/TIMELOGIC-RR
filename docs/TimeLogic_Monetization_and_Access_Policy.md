# Monetization & Access Policy
### INTEGRITY FIRST, REVENUE ON SCALE & CONTINUITY
*The governing framework for what TimeLogic charges for, what it never charges for, and why — so cost or connectivity never stands between an organization, verified proof of work, and worker equity.*

`TimeLogic — Monetization & Access Policy | 01`

---

## 01 — THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

> ### "Never let price or network connectivity lock an organization out of verified truth, worker safety, or emergency continuity."

### THE ONE-LINE POLICY
**If it guarantees proof of work, workplace safety, or offline continuity during a power or network grid failure, it is free. If it involves heavy biometric AI compute, multi-branch fleet control, enterprise ERP integrations, or deep automated penalty and payroll rules, that is where TimeLogic earns revenue.**

This is not merely an ethical stance — it is sound, scalable business. Platforms that dominate mission-critical enterprise infrastructure (e.g., Cloudflare in web resilience, Rippling and Deel in global workforce management) succeed on the exact same pattern: **a frictionless, zero-barrier entry point that builds universal operational trust and daily adoption, with high-margin revenue layered on top through compute-intensive services, hardware seat scaling, and enterprise compliance modules** — never by holding basic shift clock-ins hostage or bricking an offline terminal during an internet outage.

### THE PRACTICAL TEST
The test for any future feature, add-on, or pricing tier:
* **Does charging here risk a worker being unable to prove their presence, or an employer losing emergency attendance records during an outage?**
  * **If YES:** It stays free. We subsidize it through enterprise compute tiers or hardware licensing.
  * **If NO:** If the cost is driven by specialized GPU facial inference, multi-station hardware fleet scaling, or enterprise payroll automation, a fee is fair, sustainable, and expected.

## 02 — ALWAYS FREE, NO EXCEPTIONS

*The foundational features where a fee, paywall, or feature gate is never acceptable.*

### 1. Zero-Downtime Offline Attendance & Local Data Resilience
The moment an ISP drops, fiber cuts, or power fluctuates, attendance verification cannot halt, fail, or demand an active billing check. Local IndexedDB/SQLite outbox queuing, optimistic headcount synchronization, and automatic background batch reconciliation are permanently free. Proof of work must never depend on active broadband or billing status.

### 2. Standard PIN & Credential Verification (Universal Fallback)
Every employee has an uncompromised right to clock in. If biometric cameras are damaged, lighting conditions fail, or an employee has biometric accessibility restrictions, standard alphanumeric PIN and password manual verification is free, instant, and unlimited across all tiers.

### 3. Statutory Labor Records & Audit Data Export
An organization’s attendance and labor records belong entirely to them. Raw data exports (CSV, JSON, PDF logs), historical clock-in/out records, and basic compliance audit histories carry zero export taxes, archival fees, or retrieval paywalls.

### 4. Terminal Health Monitoring & Device Heartbeats
Basic station telemetry — monitoring whether a physical kiosk is online, tracking offline outbox queue depth, and alerting administrators to station power failures — is permanently free to ensure workplace safety and zero operational blind spots.

## 03 — WHERE THE REVENUE COMES FROM

*Charge where a real, direct cloud compute cost, hardware scaling demand, or high-value business automation sits behind the feature.*

### 1. Biometric DeepFace AI Compute & Anti-Spoofing Inference
*Pay-per-enrolled-biometric seat or high-concurrency API compute tier.*
Running 512-dimensional facial embedding extractions, cosine similarity vector lookups, and real-time anti-spoof liveness detection consumes dedicated GPU/CPU cloud infrastructure. Organizations pay for the biometric intelligence that completely eliminates buddy-punching and identity fraud.

### 2. Multi-Station Kiosk Terminal Fleet Licensing
*Per-terminal hardware seat subscription.*
Running a single central kiosk at a small office is accessible or free. Scaling across 5 warehouses, 10 retail branches, or 50 manufacturing gates requires dedicated fleet telemetry, remote station lockouts, over-the-air firmware updates, and distributed token rotation.

### 3. Dynamic Rotating QR Attendance & Cryptographic Geofencing
*High-security mobile workforce add-on.*
Sub-30-second rotating cryptographic TOTP QR codes combined with GPS geofencing radius validation and BSSID Wi-Fi handshake verification. Prevents proxy clock-ins for remote, field, and hybrid teams who do not pass through a physical hardware kiosk.

### 4. Automated Shift Penalties, Overtime & Payroll Automation
*B2B enterprise automation module.*
Multi-tier grace periods, automated tardiness salary deduction formulas, overtime calculation engines, and direct one-click payroll sync (Gusto, QuickBooks, Paystack, ADP). Businesses pay for the hundreds of manual HR reconciliation hours eliminated every month.

### 5. Multi-Branch & Multi-Tenant Enterprise Administration
*The core institutional contract tier.*
Centralized headquarters dashboard managing distributed branch locations, department-level permission hierarchies, regional holiday calendars, cross-branch floating employees, and consolidated corporate reporting.

### 6. White-Label Kiosks, Dedicated Infrastructure & 24/7 Priority SLA
*Custom enterprise contract.*
Custom corporate branding on kiosk terminal interfaces, dedicated database instances, custom data retention policies, 99.99% uptime guarantees, and dedicated solutions engineering support.

### STEP 1: Assess Continuity & Worker Rights
Ask: *Does this feature touch the essential ability to record a shift, maintain offline survival, or access historical proof of work?*
* **IF YES:** It stays free. We will never monetize panic, connectivity blackouts, or fundamental compliance data.

### STEP 2: Assess Compute, Scale & Automation Value
Ask: *Does this feature consume substantial cloud compute (GPU inference), scale across physical enterprise hardware seats, or automate complex financial workflows (payroll/penalties)?*
* **IF YES:** Price it based on measurable value created or compute consumed.

Apply this test before adding any paywall, tier, or gate across the TimeLogic ecosystem. Holding this standard before code ships protects long-term brand equity and customer retention.
